# Saint Cards Effects (PSCT)

Este documento compila las cartas nombradas en `archetype_saint_athena.md` con una redaccion PSCT en ingles lista para adaptar a EDOPro.

## Shared Mechanics

### Shared text - Bronze Cloth recursion
```text
If this face-up Equip Card in its owner's Spell & Trap Zone is sent to the GY: You can target 1 "Saint" monster you control; equip this card to that target. You can only use this effect of "[CARDNAME]" once per turn.
```

### Shared text - Bronze Cloth hand fix
```text
You can discard this card; add 1 Level 4 "Saint" monster from your Deck to your hand. You can only use this effect of "[CARDNAME]" once per turn.
```

### Shared text - Bronze Saint equip starter
```text
You can pay 500 LP; equip 1 "Cloth" Equip Spell from your hand or GY to this card. You can only use this effect of "[CARDNAME]" once per turn.
```

### Shared text - Bronze Saint material transfer
```text
If this card is sent to the GY as material for the Summon of a "Saint" monster: You can either equip 1 "Cloth" card you control to that monster, or attach 1 "Cloth" card you control to it as material (if it is an Xyz Monster). You can only use this effect of "[CARDNAME]" once per turn.
```

### Shared text - Seventh Sense (Gold Saint)
```text
(Quick Effect): You can target 1 "Cloth" card in your GY or face-up in your Spell & Trap Zone; attach it to this card as material. You can only use this effect of "[CARDNAME]" once per turn.
```

## Bronze Saints

### Saint - Seiya de Pegaso
```text
If this card is Normal or Special Summoned: You can add 1 "Cloth" Equip Spell or 1 "Saint" monster from your Deck to your hand.
If you control no monsters, or all monsters you control are "Saint" monsters: You can Special Summon this card from your hand.
You can only use each effect of "Saint - Seiya de Pegaso" once per turn.
```

### Saint - Shiryu de Dragon
```text
(Quick Effect): You can discard this card; "Cloth" cards you control cannot be destroyed by card effects this turn.
```

### Saint - Hyoga de Cisne
```text
If this card battles an opponent's monster, after damage calculation: Change that opponent's monster to Defense Position, and if you do, negate its effects until the end of your opponent's next turn.
```

### Saint - Shun de Andromeda
```text
Your opponent cannot target other "Saint" monsters you control for attacks.
If this card is equipped with a "Cloth" card, it can attack while in Defense Position. Apply its ATK for damage calculation.
```

### Saint - Ikki de Fenix
```text
If this card is in your GY: You can discard 1 "Saint" card; Special Summon this card.
You can only use this effect of "Saint - Ikki de Fenix" once per turn.
```

### Saint - Jabu de Unicornio
```text
If you control a "Saint" monster: You can Special Summon this card from your hand.
You can only Special Summon "Saint - Jabu de Unicornio" once per turn this way.
```

### Saint - Ichi de Hidra
```text
Once per turn: You can discard 1 "Cloth" card; inflict 800 damage to your opponent, and if you do, this card can attack directly this turn.
```

### Saint - Geki de Oso
```text
If this card is Normal or Special Summoned: You can add 1 Level 5 or higher "Saint" monster from your Deck to your hand.
You can only use this effect of "Saint - Geki de Oso" once per turn.
```

### Saint - Ban de Leonet
```text
If a "Saint" monster(s) you control is destroyed by battle: You can Special Summon this card from your hand or GY.
You can only use this effect of "Saint - Ban de Leonet" once per turn.
```

### Saint - Nachi de Lobo
```text
If this card is sent to the GY as Link Material or Tributed: You can draw 1 card, then discard 1 card.
You can only use this effect of "Saint - Nachi de Lobo" once per turn.
```

### Mu de Aries - El Reparador de Cloths
```text
If this card is Normal or Special Summoned: You can target up to 2 "Cloth" Equip Spells in your GY; add them to your hand.
During your Main Phase: You can discard this card; add 1 "Santuario de Athena" from your Deck to your hand.
You can only use each effect of "Mu de Aries - El Reparador de Cloths" once per turn.
```

### Kiki - El Mensajero del Escultor de Armaduras
```text
(Quick Effect): You can discard this card, then target 1 "Saint" monster you control; equip 1 "Cloth" Equip Spell from your Deck or GY to that target.
During the Standby Phase of the next turn after this card was sent to the GY: You can banish this card; add up to 2 "Cloth" cards with different names from your GY to your hand.
You can only use each effect of "Kiki - El Mensajero del Escultor de Armaduras" once per turn.
```

## Silver Saints

You can only use this effect of "Silver Saint - Marin de Aguila" once per turn.

## Gold Saints

```text
2+ "Saint" monsters
This card gains 500 ATK for each Equip Card on the field.
Once per turn (Quick Effect): You can target 1 "Cloth" Equip Spell in your GY; equip it to this card.
This card gains the effects of "Saint" monsters currently equipped with their corresponding "Cloth" cards.
```

### Gold Saint - Mu de Aries
```text
You can detach 1 material; add 1 "Cloth" card from your GY to your hand, or if that card was a "Gold Cloth", you can equip it to a monster you control instead.
You can only use this effect of "Gold Saint - Mu de Aries" once per turn.
```

### Gold Saint - Aiolia de Leo
```text
Once per turn: You can detach any number of materials from this card; destroy up to that many monsters your opponent controls.
```

### Gold Saint - Shaka de Virgo
```text
While this card has a "Gold Cloth" card as material, your opponent cannot activate card effects in the GY, also they cannot banish cards.
Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card; negate the effects of all face-up cards currently on the field until the end of this turn.
```

### Gold Saint - Saga de Geminis
```text
(Quick Effect): You can detach 2 materials from this card; destroy all cards in 1 column, and if you do, inflict 1000 damage to your opponent.
You can only use this effect of "Gold Saint - Saga de Geminis" once per turn.
```

### Gold Saint - Dohko de Libra - Maestro de los Cinco Picos
```text
This card gains these effects based on the number of materials attached to it.
● 1+: Cannot be destroyed by battle.
● 2+: Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card; destroy 1 Spell/Trap on the field.
● 3+: (Quick Effect): You can detach 1 material from this card; "Saint" monsters you control gain 1000 ATK and cannot be targeted by your opponent's card effects, until the end of this turn.
```

### Gold Saint - Aldebaran de Tauro
```text
Once per turn, when an opponent's monster declares an attack: You can detach 1 material from this card; negate that attack, and if you do, destroy that monster, then inflict 1000 damage to your opponent.
```

### Gold Saint - Mascara de Muerte de Cancer
```text
Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card, then target up to 2 monsters in your opponent's GY; banish them, and if you do, this card gains 300 ATK for each card banished by this effect.
```

### Gold Saint - Milo de Escorpio
```text
Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card, then target 1 face-up monster on the field; place 1 "Scarlet Needle Counter" on it.
Monsters with 3 or more "Scarlet Needle Counters" are sent to the GY.
```

### Gold Saint - Shura de Capricornio
```text
Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card, then target 1 card on the field; send it to the GY.
```

### Gold Saint - Afrodita de Piscis
```text
Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card; your opponent cannot declare attacks during their next Battle Phase.
```

### Gold Saint - Aiolos de Sagitario
```text
Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card; this card gains ATK equal to the combined original ATK of all "Bronze Saint" monsters in your GY, until the End Phase.
```

### Gold Saint - Camus de Acuario
```text
Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card, then target 2 cards your opponent controls; while this card is face-up on the field, those cards cannot be activated, cannot change their battle positions, and cannot attack.
```

## Bronze Cloth (Equip Spells)

### Bronze Cloth - Pegaso
```text
Equip only to a "Saint" monster.
The equipped monster gains 500 ATK.
If the equipped monster attacks, your opponent cannot activate cards or effects until the end of the Damage Step.
If the equipped monster is "Saint - Seiya de Pegaso", it can make up to 2 attacks on monsters during each Battle Phase, also if it destroys an opponent's monster by battle: Inflict 500 damage to your opponent.
```

### Bronze Cloth - Dragon
```text
Equip only to a "Saint" monster.
The equipped monster gains 1000 DEF.
The equipped monster cannot be destroyed by monster effects.
If the equipped monster is "Saint - Shiryu de Dragon", your opponent cannot target it with card effects.
Once per turn, if the equipped monster in Defense Position would be destroyed by battle, it is not destroyed, and if you do, you can destroy 1 card your opponent controls.
```

### Bronze Cloth - Cisne
```text
Equip only to a "Saint" monster.
Once per turn: You can target 1 face-up card your opponent controls; negate its effects until the end of this turn.
If the equipped monster is "Saint - Hyoga de Cisne", monsters negated by this card's effect cannot change their battle positions, also they cannot be used as material for a Special Summon from the Extra Deck while this card is face-up on the field.
```

### Bronze Cloth - Andromeda
```text
Equip only to a "Saint" monster.
Your opponent cannot activate Trap Cards during the Battle Phase.
The equipped monster can attack directly.
If the equipped monster is "Saint - Shun de Andromeda", while it is in Defense Position, your opponent cannot declare attacks on other monsters you control, also they cannot activate the effects of monsters that were Special Summoned this turn.
```

### Bronze Cloth - Fenix
```text
Equip only to a "Saint" monster.
The equipped monster gains 1000 ATK.
If the equipped monster destroys an opponent's monster by battle: Inflict 1000 damage to your opponent.
If the equipped monster is "Saint - Ikki de Fenix", and it would be sent to the GY: You can destroy this card instead, and if you do, Special Summon that monster, then you can destroy 1 card on the field.
```

### Bronze Cloth - Unicornio
```text
Equip only to a "Saint" monster.
The equipped monster can make a second attack during each Battle Phase, but only on monsters.
If the equipped monster is "Saint - Jabu de Unicornio", you gain this effect.
● During your Main Phase, you can Normal Summon 1 "Saint" monster in addition to your Normal Summon/Set. (You can only gain this effect once per turn.)
```

### Bronze Cloth - Hidra
```text
Equip only to a "Saint" monster.
If an opponent's monster battles the equipped monster, after damage calculation: That opponent's monster loses 1000 ATK/DEF.
If the equipped monster is "Saint - Ichi de Hidra", and it attacks directly, your opponent cannot activate effects in the GY until the end of this turn.
```

### Bronze Cloth - Oso
```text
Equip only to a "Saint" monster.
If the equipped monster destroys an opponent's monster by battle: Your opponent discards 1 random card.
If the equipped monster is "Saint - Geki de Oso", at the start of the Damage Step, if it battles an opponent's monster with higher ATK: You can destroy that opponent's monster.
```

## Spells and Traps

### Santuario de Athena (Field Spell - base)
```text
All "Saint" monsters on the field gain 300 ATK/DEF.
Once per turn, if a "Saint" monster you control would be destroyed, you can send 1 "Cloth" Equip Card equipped to it to the GY instead.
```

### Santuario de Athena (Field Spell - v2)
```text
All "Saint" monsters on the field gain 300 ATK/DEF.
You can only use each of the following effects of "Santuario de Athena" once per turn.
● When this card is activated: You can add 1 Level 4 or lower "Saint" monster from your Deck to your hand.
● If a "Saint" monster you control would be destroyed by battle or card effect, you can send 1 "Cloth" card equipped to it to the GY instead.
● If 1 or more "Cloth" cards were sent to your GY this turn: You can apply this effect; during the Standby Phase of your next turn, add up to 2 of those cards from your GY to your hand, or equip them to "Saint" monsters you control.
```

### Eleva tu Cosmos! (Normal Spell)
```text
Send 1 "Saint" monster from your Deck to the GY; add 1 "Saint" monster with a different name from your Deck to your hand.
You can only activate 1 "Eleva tu Cosmos!" per turn.
```

### Atenea Exclamation (Counter Trap)
```text
If you control 3 or more "Saint" monsters with different names: When your opponent activates a card or effect: Negate the activation, and if you do, destroy that card.
```

### El Torneo Galactico (Continuous Spell)
```text
Each time a "Saint" monster is Special Summoned, place 1 Cosmos Counter on this card (max. 5).
While this card has 3 or more Cosmos Counters, your "Bronze Saint" monsters cannot be destroyed by card effects.
You can send this card with 5 Cosmos Counters to the GY; Special Summon as many Level 4 or lower "Saint" monsters as possible from your GY, but negate their effects, also destroy them during the End Phase.
```

### Orden de Asesinato del Santuario (Normal Spell)
```text
Target 1 "Silver Saint" monster you control and 1 monster your opponent controls; destroy them, and if you do, you can Special Summon 1 "Bronze Saint" monster from your Deck.
If this card is in your GY: You can banish this card; add 1 "Silver Cloth" card from your Deck to your hand.
You can only use this GY effect of "Orden de Asesinato del Santuario" once per turn.
```

### Herencia de Oro (Normal Spell)
```text
Target 1 "Bronze Saint" monster you control; equip 1 "Gold Cloth" Equip Spell from your Deck or GY to that target.
Immediately after this effect resolves, Special Summon 1 "Gold Saint" Xyz Monster from your Extra Deck, by using that target as material. (This is treated as an Xyz Summon. Transfer its materials to the Summoned monster.)
If this card is in your GY: You can banish this card, then target 1 "Gold Saint" Xyz Monster you control; attach 1 "Cloth" card from your GY to it as material.
You can only use this GY effect of "Herencia de Oro" once per turn.
```

### El Despertar del Cosmos (Quick-Play Spell)
```text
Target 1 "Saint" monster you control; this turn, it can be treated as 2 materials for the Xyz Summon of a "Saint" monster, or as a Tuner for the Synchro Summon of a "Silver Saint" monster.
If a "Saint" monster you control would be destroyed, you can banish this card from your GY instead.
You can only activate 1 "El Despertar del Cosmos" per turn.
```

### Legado del Maestro (Normal Spell)
```text
Reveal up to 3 "Cloth" cards in your hand, shuffle them into the Deck, then draw that many cards + 1.
If you control no "Saint" monsters, while this card is in your GY: You can banish this card; Special Summon 1 "Bronze Saint" monster from your Deck.
You can only use this effect of "Legado del Maestro" once per turn.
```
