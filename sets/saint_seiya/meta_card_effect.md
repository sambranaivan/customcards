# Meta / Generic Staples (PSCT)

Este documento compila las cartas **genéricas faction-neutral** del formato Saint Seiya, pensadas para funcionar en cualquier deck (Saint, Poseidon, Specter/Hades, God Warrior, Steel, Black Saint, etc.) sin pertenecer a un arquetipo específico. Todas las cartas están reskineadas con lore universal del anime (Cosmos, Octavo Sentido, dioses del Olimpo, Moiras, Chronos, Tártaro, estrellas, etc.) y están escritas con redacción PSCT en inglés lista para adaptar a EDOPro.

---

## 1. Draw & Card Advantage

### Revelation of the Cosmos
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Pay 1000 LP; draw 2 cards, then, if you control no monsters, you cannot Special Summon monsters from the Extra Deck for the rest of this turn.
You can only activate 1 "Revelation of the Cosmos" per turn.
```
Flavor: el instante en que un guerrero percibe el universo entero. El pago de LP representa el desgaste espiritual de expandir el Cosmos.

### Omen of the Stars
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
At the start of your Main Phase 1, banish 3 or 6 random cards from your Extra Deck, face-down; draw 1 card for every 3 cards banished. For the rest of this turn after this card resolves, you cannot activate other "Pot" cards, also negate any other effects of cards drawn by this effect.
You can only activate 1 "Omen of the Stars" per turn.
```
Flavor: leer el mapa estelar sacrifica el futuro (Extra Deck) para ganar presente.

### Three Paths of Destiny
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Excavate the top 3 cards of your Deck, add 1 of them to your hand, then place the rest on the bottom of your Deck in any order. You cannot Special Summon monsters during the turn you activate this card.
You can only activate 1 "Three Paths of Destiny" per turn.
```
Flavor: las Moiras tejiendo tres hilos; eliges un futuro a costa de renunciar al poder.

### Blessing of the Moirai
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Draw 3 cards, then discard 2 cards.
You can only activate 1 "Blessing of the Moirai" per turn.
```
Flavor: Cloto, Láquesis y Átropos te entregan tres hilos; debes cortar dos.

### Pact with the Foundation
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Draw 1 card. Your opponent gains 1000 LP.
You can only activate 1 "Pact with the Foundation" per turn.
```
Flavor: el dinero de la Fundación (Kido/Graude) acelerando la misión; el rival también gana recursos por la deuda de sangre contraída.

## 2. Monster Removal

### Wrath of Zeus
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Destroy all monsters your opponent controls. You cannot conduct your Battle Phase the turn you activate this card.
You can only activate 1 "Wrath of Zeus" per turn.
```
Flavor: el rayo del padre de los dioses. Tan devastador que paraliza incluso al invocador.

### Cosmic Singularity
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Destroy all monsters on the field. Monsters destroyed by this effect cannot be Special Summoned from the GY for the rest of this turn.
You can only activate 1 "Cosmic Singularity" per turn.
```
Flavor: un agujero negro colapsando todas las constelaciones a la vez.

### Meteor of Judgment
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Tribute 1 monster your opponent controls; Special Summon 1 "Divine Meteor Token" (Rock/EARTH/Level 8/ATK 2500/DEF 2500) to your opponent's field in Defense Position. That Token cannot be Tributed, also its effects cannot be activated or applied. While that Token is on the field, neither player can activate the effects of monsters with the same Type as the Tributed monster.
You can only activate 1 "Meteor of Judgment" per turn.
```
Flavor: los meteoritos que anuncian el juicio divino sustituyen una amenaza por otra más pesada e inmóvil.

### Titan's Strike
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Destroy 1 face-up monster your opponent controls with the highest DEF (your choice, if tied).
```
Flavor: los Titanes golpeando la tierra con puños de montaña; derriban al más estable del campo.

### Storm of Olympus
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Discard 1 card; destroy all face-up monsters your opponent controls.
You can only activate 1 "Storm of Olympus" per turn.
```
Flavor: la descarga eléctrica del monte sagrado arrasa a quienes se exponen.

## 3. Spell / Trap Removal

### Stellar Exile
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Pay 1000 LP, then target 1 Spell/Trap on the field; banish it.
```
Flavor: una estrella fugaz arrastra la amenaza a otra dimensión.

### Gale of Tartarus
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Destroy all Spells and Traps your opponent controls. You cannot activate other Spell/Trap Cards the turn you activate this card, except Normal Spells.
You can only activate 1 "Gale of Tartarus" per turn.
```
Flavor: vientos del inframundo griego arrasando con ataduras y trampas.

### Wind Prayer
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Discard 1 card, then target up to 2 Spells/Traps on the field; destroy them.
```
Flavor: una plegaria breve que invoca a Eolo; dos ráfagas cortan la escena.

### Starfall
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Target 1 Spell/Trap on the field; destroy that target.
```
Flavor: la lluvia de estrellas que cae silenciosa sobre el Santuario; el staple "MST" neutral del formato.

## 4. Negation & Counter

### Verdict of the Gods
- **Card Type**: Counter Trap

```text
When a monster effect is activated, or when a monster(s) would be Summoned: Pay 1500 LP; negate the activation or the Summon, and if you do, destroy that card.
```
Flavor: un pequeño consejo divino castiga intervenciones individuales.

### Judgment of Olympus
- **Card Type**: Counter Trap

```text
When a monster would be Summoned, OR a Spell/Trap Card is activated: Pay half your LP; negate the Summon or activation, and if you do, destroy that card.
```
Flavor: los doce dioses del Olimpo dictan sentencia absoluta. El costo es ruinoso.

### Whisper from the Beyond
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Target 1 monster in your opponent's GY; banish it, and if you do, until the end of the next turn, its effects are negated, as well as the activated effects and effects on the field of monsters with the same name.
```
Flavor: las almas del Yomotsu/Hades silencian a los muertos antes de que vuelvan.

### Decree of Chronos
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Declare 1 card name; banish 1 card with that name from your Deck, and if you do, for the rest of this turn, negate all activated effects and effects on the field of monsters with that original name, also negate the effects of Spells/Traps with that name.
```
Flavor: Chronos, el tiempo mismo, borra una entidad de la línea temporal.

### Seal of the Ancient Sanctuary
- **Card Type**: Continuous Trap

```text
Both players must Set Spell Cards before activating them, and cannot activate them until their next turn.
```
Flavor: la neutralidad del Santuario original antes del conflicto; ralentiza a todos por igual.

## 5. Revival & Recursion

### Awakening of the Eighth Sense
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Target 1 monster in either player's GY; Special Summon it.
You can only activate 1 "Awakening of the Eighth Sense" per turn.
```
Flavor: el Octavo Sentido es universal a toda facción; al despertar, un alma regresa del Yomotsu al mundo.

### Echo of the Cosmos
- **Card Type**: Continuous Trap

```text
Activate this card by targeting 1 monster in your GY; Special Summon it. When this card leaves the field, destroy that monster. When that monster is destroyed, destroy this card.
```
Flavor: el eco cósmico de un guerrero caído lo trae de vuelta mientras su memoria persista.

### Seed of Yggdrasil
- **Card Type**: Normal Spell

```text
Discard 1 monster; Special Summon 1 Level 1 monster from your hand or Deck.
You can only activate 1 "Seed of Yggdrasil" per turn.
```
Flavor: el árbol cósmico brota de una semilla; sacrificas una rama madura para hacer nacer un brote.

## 6. Battle Tricks & Protection

### Eclipse of Selene
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Target 1 face-up monster on the field; change it to face-down Defense Position.
```
Flavor: la diosa lunar oculta al guerrero bajo su manto nocturno.

### Chalice of the Gods
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Target 1 face-up Effect Monster on the field; until the end of this turn, its effects are negated, but it gains 400 ATK.
```
Flavor: ambrosía divina: enloquece de poder pero silencia la técnica.

### Spear of Destiny
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Target 1 face-up monster on the field; until the end of this turn, that target is unaffected by other Spells/Traps, but loses 800 ATK.
```
Flavor: una lanza forjada por las Moiras perfora todo hechizo, a costa de la fuerza del portador.

### Law of Gravity
- **Card Type**: Quick-Play Spell

```text
Target 1 face-up monster on the field; that target's original ATK is halved until the end of this turn.
```
Flavor: el peso del cosmos cae sobre el guerrero; su golpe más fuerte se reduce a la mitad.

### Cosmos Wall
- **Card Type**: Normal Trap

```text
Your opponent cannot declare an attack this turn.
```
Flavor: un muro invisible tejido con Cosmos puro que detiene la Battle Phase enemiga.

## 7. Hand Disruption

### Cosmo Intrusion
- **Card Type**: Normal Trap

```text
Declare 1 card name; your opponent reveals their hand, and if the declared card is there, they discard all copies. Otherwise, you discard 1 random card from your hand.
```
Flavor: un guerrero leyendo la voluntad del rival (como Saga leyendo intenciones). Errar tiene costo.

## 8. Floodgates (Side)

### Mist of Limbo
- **Card Type**: Continuous Trap

```text
Activate this card by paying 1000 LP. Negate the effects of all face-up Effect Monsters on the field (but their effects can still be activated).
```
Flavor: el limbo donde las almas pierden sus poderes; equivalente neutral a Skill Drain.

### Stellar Prison
- **Card Type**: Continuous Trap

```text
Any card sent to the GY is banished instead.
```
Flavor: las estrellas como cárceles que atrapan las almas antes de llegar al inframundo.

## 9. Field Spell

### Galaxia
- **Card Type**: Field Spell

```text
All monsters on the field gain 200 ATK/DEF for each different Attribute among monsters on the field (max 1000 ATK/DEF).
Once per turn, during your Main Phase: You can send 1 monster from your hand to the GY; draw 1 card.
You can only control 1 "Galaxia".
```
Flavor: el universo como terreno común para Saints, Marinas, Specters y Guerreros Divinos.

## 10. Generic Handtraps

### Cosmic Intervention
- **Card Type**: Effect Monster
- **Attribute**: LIGHT
- **Type**: Fairy
- **Level**: 3
- **ATK/DEF**: 0 / 1800

```text
When a card or effect is activated that includes any of these effects (Quick Effect): You can discard this card; negate that effect.
- Add a card from the Deck to the hand.
- Special Summon from the Deck.
- Send a card from the Deck to the GY.
You can only use this effect of "Cosmic Intervention" once per turn.
```
Flavor: la voz del universo interrumpe al que intenta quebrar el equilibrio.

### Chronos' Pause
- **Card Type**: Effect Monster / Tuner
- **Attribute**: LIGHT
- **Type**: Spellcaster
- **Level**: 1
- **ATK/DEF**: 0 / 0

```text
During your opponent's Main Phase (Quick Effect): You can send this card from your hand to the GY, then target 1 Effect Monster your opponent controls; negate its effects until the end of this turn.
You can only use this effect of "Chronos' Pause" once per turn.
```
Flavor: un parpadeo en el flujo del tiempo deja al enemigo sin técnica por un turno.

### Petrifying Gaze
- **Card Type**: Normal Trap

```text
Target 1 Effect Monster your opponent controls; negate its effects (until the end of this turn), then, if this card was activated from your hand, for the rest of this turn after this card resolves, other Spell/Trap effects in this column cannot be activated.
If you control no cards, you can activate this card from your hand.
```
Flavor: la mirada de la Gorgona griega, un mito anterior a toda facción; petrifica al instante.

### Big Bang Collapse
- **Card Type**: Effect Monster
- **Attribute**: LIGHT
- **Type**: Rock
- **Level**: 11
- **ATK/DEF**: 3000 / 600

```text
During your opponent's Main Phase, if your opponent Normal or Special Summoned 5 or more monsters this turn (Quick Effect): You can Tribute as many monsters on the field as possible, and if you do, Special Summon this card from your hand, then Special Summon 1 "Primordial Cosmos Token" (Rock/LIGHT/Level 11/ATK ?/DEF ?) to your opponent's field, whose original ATK/DEF become the combined original ATK/DEF of the monsters Tributed by this effect.
You can only use this effect of "Big Bang Collapse" once per turn.
```
Flavor: el evento de origen del universo castigando a quien extiende demasiado la creación.

## Design Notes

- **Faction-neutral**: ninguna de estas cartas menciona nombres de arquetipos (`Saint`, `Pillar`, `Specter`, `God Warrior`, `Cloth`, etc.). Cualquier deck del formato puede correrlas sin choques de nombres.
- **Lore universal**: se utilizan conceptos compartidos por toda la cosmología de Saint Seiya (Cosmos, Octavo Sentido, Olimpo, Moiras, Tártaro, Chronos, Yggdrasil como concepto cósmico, estrellas/constelaciones).
- **Balance**: `Cosmic Singularity`, `Judgment of Olympus` y `Big Bang Collapse` son las tres cartas más volátiles; probablemente requieran límite de 1 o 2 copias en la lista oficial del formato.
- **Setcode sugerido**: al registrar en `cards-unofficial.cdb`, asignar un setcode común (p. ej. `0xXYZ` = "Cosmos"/"Meta") para que cada deck pueda identificarlas como staples del formato sin mezclarlas con su arquetipo propio.
- **Relación con `Saints_card_new_supports.md`**: ese archivo contiene staples "Saint-flavored" (Cometa de Pegaso, Tormenta Nebular, handtraps de Marin/Shaina/Cassios, etc.) que están ligados al arquetipo Saint. Este `meta_card_effect.md` provee las versiones **neutrales equivalentes** que los demás decks pueden usar.
