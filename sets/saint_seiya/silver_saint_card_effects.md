# Silver Saints - Mandato del Patriarca (PSCT)

Documento para una variante **deck solo Silver** de enfoque **Control/Opresion**, inspirada en la saga del Santuario cuando los Silver Saints obedecen al Patriarca.

En esta version, los Silver Saints son **Effect Monsters de Main Deck** (no Synchro), con soporte central de cartas "Edicto"/"Mandato" y boss final `Patriarca Ares`.

---

## Core Rule del Subarquetipo

- Casi todos los Silver Saints comparten esta clausula:
  - "If you control 'Patriarca Ares' or a 'Mandato del Patriarca' card, you can Special Summon this card from your hand."
- Esto permite jugarlos sin Bronze Saints y sostener identidad de "escuadron represivo".

---

## Command Unit

### Patriarca Ares - Voz del Santuario
- **Card Type**: Effect Monster
- **Attribute**: DARK
- **Type**: Spellcaster
- **Level**: 4
- **ATK/DEF**: 1000 / 1800

```text
If this card is Normal or Special Summoned: You can add 1 "Mandato del Patriarca" card from your Deck to your hand, except "Patriarca Ares - Voz del Santuario".
While this card is face-up on the field, your opponent cannot target other "Silver Saint" monsters you control with card effects.
If this card is sent from the field to the GY by an opponent's card: You can Special Summon 1 "Silver Saint" monster from your hand or GY, except "Patriarca Ares - Voz del Santuario".
During your Main Phase: You can banish 1 "Silver Saint" monster from your GY; Special Summon this card from your GY.
You can only use each effect of "Patriarca Ares - Voz del Santuario" once per turn.
```

### Mandato del Patriarca - Cadena de Mando

```text

Once per turn: You can reveal 1 "Silver Saint" monster in your hand; Special Summon 1 "Silver Saint" monster with a different name from your hand.
```

## Deck Concept (solo Silver)

- **Main Engine**: 14-18 Silver Effect Monsters.
- **Bosses**: 2-3 copias de `Patriarca Ares - Usurpador del Santuario`.
- **Command Package**: 8-12 cartas "Mandato del Patriarca".
- **Plan**: abrir con negaciones parciales, bloquear recursos del rival y cerrar partida bajo control de Ares.
