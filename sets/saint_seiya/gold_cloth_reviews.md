# Gold Cloth — Synergy Effects Review (PSCT)

Each Gold Cloth currently has a generic effect available to any "Gold Saint" monster.
This document proposes a dedicated synergy effect for each Cloth when equipped to its
corresponding Gold Saint, reinforcing the thematic identity of each pairing.

Two Cloths already have synergy effects (Sagittarius, Pisces); they are included here
for completeness and reviewed for balance.

---

## Format

Each entry shows:
- Current generic effect (unchanged)
- **Proposed synergy line** to add when equipped to the matching Gold Saint
- Balance notes

---

## Gold Cloth - Aries
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Mu of Aries**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
The equipped monster gains 1000 DEF.
Once per turn, if a face-up "Saint" card(s) you control would be destroyed by battle or
card effect, it is not destroyed.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Mu of Aries": Once per turn, when a
"Cloth" card is sent to your GY by a card effect: You can attach it to the equipped monster
as Xyz Material instead of sending it there.
```

> **Design intent**: Mu's role is cloth recovery. This synergy feeds his material engine
> passively — every Cloth that would hit the GY becomes a new material. Thematically
> represents his telekinetic repair ability.
>
> **Balance**: Conditional trigger (only when a Cloth is sent by effect, not by being
> equipped). Does not generate advantage on its own; requires the opponent or the game
> state to trigger it.

## Gold Cloth - Taurus
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Aldebaran of Taurus**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
The equipped monster gains 1000 ATK/DEF.
Your opponent cannot target other "Saint" monsters you control for attacks.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Aldebaran of Taurus": Once per turn,
when an opponent's monster declares an attack while the equipped monster has no Xyz
Material: You can negate that attack and destroy that attacking monster, without detaching
a material.
```

> **Design intent**: Aldebaran without materials loses his negate-attack ability. This
> synergy keeps him functional when exhausted of materials, representing his endurance as
> the mightiest of the Gold Saints physically.
>
> **Balance**: Only activates when he has 0 materials, so it is a fallback — not a
> replacement for the main effect. Strictly once per turn.

## Gold Cloth - Gemini
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Saga of Gemini**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
The equipped monster gains 800 ATK.
Once per turn, when a card or effect is activated that would affect the equipped monster:
You can negate that effect.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Saga of Gemini": The equipped monster's
Quick Effect that destroys cards in a column requires detaching only 1 material instead of
2. If it does, it does not inflict damage this turn.
```

> **Design intent**: Saga's column destruction costs 2 materials (heavy). With his Cloth,
> the cost halves — but you lose the 1000 burn, keeping the total power the same. Reflects
> Galaxian Explosion becoming more controlled with his armor.
>
> **Balance**: Straight power reduction in cost, but removes the burn damage as
> compensation. Net result: more activations, less burst damage. Fair trade-off.

## Gold Cloth - Cancer
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Deathmask of Cancer**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
The equipped monster is also treated as a Zombie monster.
If a monster(s) would be sent to your opponent's GY, banish it instead.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Deathmask of Cancer": When the
equipped monster activates its effect that banishes monsters from your opponent's GY,
it gains 500 ATK for each banished monster instead of 300. Cards banished by this effect
cannot be returned to the field or GY by card effects.
```

> **Design intent**: Deathmask's ATK gain is modest (300 per card). With his own Cloth,
> the gain increases to 500 and the banished monsters are permanently removed (no
> "Burial from a Different Dimension" recovery). Represents sending souls to the
> underworld with no return.
>
> **Balance**: Higher gain rewards using the matching Cloth. The permanent banish is a
> meaningful upgrade but only applies to GY monsters already targeted by his effect.

## Gold Cloth - Leo
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Aiolia of Leo**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
The equipped monster gains 800 ATK.
If the equipped monster attacks, your opponent cannot activate effects until the end of
the Damage Step.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Aiolia of Leo": When the equipped
monster destroys opponent's monsters by its own effect, inflict 500 damage to your
opponent for each monster destroyed by that effect.
```

> **Design intent**: Aiolia's Lightning Plasma destroys multiple monsters at once.
> With his Cloth, each destroyed monster deals 500 burn, rewarding mass destruction.
> Two monsters = 1000 damage; four = 2000. Reflects the heat and impact of his cosmos.
>
> **Balance**: The burn only triggers off his own detach effect (not generic battle
> destruction). Requires investing materials to get the damage, so it is not free.

## Gold Cloth - Virgo
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Shaka of Virgo**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
The equipped monster is unaffected by your opponent's activated monster effects.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Shaka of Virgo": Once per turn,
when your opponent Special Summons a monster(s) from the Extra Deck: You can negate
the Summon(s), and if you do, banish that monster(s).
```

> **Design intent**: Shaka's passive (GY/banish floodgate) already applies when he has a
> Gold Cloth as Xyz Material. When his Cloth is physically equipped instead, he gains
> Extra Deck Summon negation — the highest level of gate-keeping, fitting for the Gold
> Saint closest to enlightenment.
>
> **Balance**: Requires the Cloth to be equipped (not as material), so the two forms of
> his floodgate are mutually exclusive. Negating Extra Deck Summons is strong but
> strictly once per turn and costs nothing beyond having the Cloth equipped. Counterplay:
> destroying the Equip Spell removes this ability.

## Gold Cloth - Libra
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Dohko of Libra - Master of the Five Ancient Peaks**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
The equipped monster gains 500 ATK.
It can attack while in Defense Position. Apply its ATK for damage calculation.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Dohko of Libra - Master of the Five
Ancient Peaks": Once per turn, when the equipped monster would detach a material to
activate one of its effects, you can banish 1 "Cloth" card from your GY instead of
detaching that material.
```

> **Design intent**: Dohko's power scales with the number of materials. This synergy lets
> him "spend" from the GY instead of his material stack, preserving his tier of effects
> for longer. Represents the Five Peaks endurance — conserving cosmos instead of spending
> it.
>
> **Balance**: Requires having Cloth cards in the GY (not free). Only substitutes 1
> material cost per turn, does not grant extra activations. The GY is a finite resource.

## Gold Cloth - Scorpio
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Milo of Scorpio**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
If the equipped monster battles an opponent's monster, before damage calculation:
Destroy that opponent's monster.
If the equipped monster destroys a monster by battle: Inflict 1500 damage to your opponent.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Milo of Scorpio": When the equipped
monster places a "Scarlet Needle Counter" on a monster, place 1 additional "Scarlet Needle
Counter" on that same monster. Also, the threshold to send monsters to the GY is reduced
to 2 or more "Scarlet Needle Counters" instead of 3.
```

> **Design intent**: Milo is underpowered without his Cloth (3 turns to destroy 1
> monster). With Scorpio equipped, each activation places 2 counters and the threshold
> drops to 2 — meaning 1 activation destroys a monster immediately, or 2 activations
> destroy two monsters simultaneously. This brings Milo in line with other Rank 4s.
>
> **Balance**: Requires equipping the specific Cloth. Without it, Milo remains slow by
> design. The Scorpio Cloth's battle destruction already handles combat; this synergy
> makes his effect useful in situations where battle is not an option.

## Gold Cloth - Capricorn
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Shura of Capricorn**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
The equipped monster's attacks cannot be negated.
If the equipped monster destroys an opponent's monster by battle, it can make a second
attack in a row.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Shura of Capricorn": When the
equipped monster activates its effect to send a card on the field to the GY, you can
target 1 additional card on the field. Cards sent to the GY by the equipped monster's
effects cannot be activated in response to, and their effects are negated when sent.
```

> **Design intent**: Shura's Excalibur cuts through anything. With his Cloth, his removal
> effect hits 2 cards at once and the sent cards cannot respond (no "in response"
> activation). Reflects the clean, instant cut of Excalibur.
>
> **Balance**: Double removal is strong, but costs 1 material for both targets (no
> additional detach). The "cannot respond" clause prevents chaining but does not change
> the quantity of cards removed per material spent.

## Gold Cloth - Aquarius
**Paired with: Gold Saint - Camus of Aquarius**

```
Equip only to a "Gold Saint" monster.
Monsters your opponent controls lose 500 ATK.
If a monster battles the equipped monster, at the end of the Damage Step: Change that
monster to face-down Defense Position.
[SYNERGY] If this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Camus of Aquarius": Cards frozen by
the equipped monster's effect (that cannot be activated or change battle positions) also
cannot be Tributed, also monsters among those cards cannot be used as material for any
Summon.
```

> **Design intent**: Camus's "Aurora Execution" freezes cards but the opponent can still
> use them as tribute or material costs. With his Cloth, the frozen cards are completely
> locked — they exist on the field but cannot be used in any way. True absolute zero.
>
> **Balance**: This upgrades the quality of the existing freeze, not the quantity. The
> opponent can still remove the frozen cards with effects that target or destroy (not
> "activate") them, and destroying Camus ends all freeze effects.

## Already Implemented (Reference)

### Gold Cloth - Sagittarius ✓
```
[SYNERGY] If the equipped monster is "Saint - Seiya of Pegasus" or
"Gold Saint - Aiolos of Sagittarius", when it declares an attack, your opponent cannot
activate cards or effects until the end of the Battle Phase.
```
> Status: **Implemented. OK.** Note: combined with Aiolos's broken ATK gain, this synergy
> should be reviewed after fixing Aiolos's base effect.

---

### Gold Cloth - Pisces ✓
```
[SYNERGY] While this card is equipped to "Gold Saint - Aphrodite of Pisces", your
opponent must keep their hand revealed.
```
> Status: **Implemented. OK.** Thematic and balanced — information advantage without hard
> negation.
